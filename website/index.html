Chapter 03 — The solution

The five signs dermatologists teach.

DermoAI puts the ABCDE rule in your pocket. One of these on a spot — or a spot that simply worries you — is reason to book a consultation.

A

Asymmetry

One half doesn't match the other.

B

Border

Irregular, ragged or notched edges.

C

Colour

More than one shade, or uneven colour.

D

Diameter

Bigger than a pencil eraser (~6 mm).

E

Evolving

Changing in size, shape or colour — the one to act on.

Timeline

Every scan, dated and compared, so change is visible at a glance.

SkinMind

30-second daily check-ins that connect your skin, sleep and stress.

Treatment routines

Build a routine with steps you tick off — morning and evening.

Find a dermatologist

Clinics near you from OpenStreetMap, with directions and call buttons.

Doctor Report

A clean PDF of your scans and trends to bring to an appointment.

Ask DermoAI

A built-in assistant that knows skin health and the app itself.

Privacy

Your skin stays on your phone.

Scan photos, your skin profile and your journal are stored on-device only. Nothing is uploaded to DermoAI servers, and even the AI assistant runs on your own API key.

  • Scans are stored locally, never on our servers
  • No account required to look at a spot
  • Your location only leaves the app when you search for a clinic
  • You can delete any scan — and its photo — at any time
