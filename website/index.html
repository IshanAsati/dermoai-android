How it works

Three steps, one honest loop.

DermoAI never diagnoses. It helps you see clearly and track change — the two things that matter most.

01 — Capture

Photograph a spot

The app frames your skin in good light and lets you crop precisely. Your photo stays on your phone.

02 — Analyze

Read it in plain language

An on-device model estimates severity and confidence, and explains what each sign may mean — no uploads, no account needed to look.

03 — Track

Watch what changes

A timeline, daily check-ins and a doctor-ready PDF report show you whether anything is evolving over time.

The ABCDE rule

The five signs dermatologists teach.

DermoAI puts them in your pocket. One of these on a mole or spot — or a spot that simply worries you — is reason to book a consultation.

A

Asymmetry

One half doesn't match the other.

B

Border

Irregular, ragged or notched edges.

C

Colour

More than one shade, or uneven colour.

D

Diameter

Bigger than a pencil eraser (~6 mm).

E

Evolving

Changing in size, shape or colour — the one to act on.

Privacy

Your skin stays on your phone.

Scan photos, your skin profile and your journal are stored on-device only. Nothing is uploaded to DermoAI servers, and even the AI assistant runs on your own API key.

  • Scans are stored locally, never on our servers
  • No account required to look at a spot
  • Your location only leaves the app when you search for a clinic
  • You can delete any scan — and its photo — at any time
